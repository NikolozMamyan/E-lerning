2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, improve user experience, and provide information to site owners.

3. Types of Cookies Used

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the proper functioning of the website (e.g., authentication, security, account access).
  • Performance and Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the website to improve functionality and content (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and choices (e.g., language, region).
  • Advertising Cookies: May be used to show relevant ads or limit their repetition (only with your consent).

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services integrated into our site, such as:

  • Payment providers (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Analytics tools (Google Analytics)
  • Video hosting platforms (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo)

We have no control over these cookies and recommend reviewing their respective policies.

5. Cookie Management

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect the website’s functionality.

Instructions for major browsers:

  • Google Chrome

  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6. Consent

When you first visit our site, a cookie banner informs you about the use of cookies and allows you to accept or customize your preferences. You can change your consent at any time from the site’s footer link.

7. Data Collected Through Cookies

Cookies may collect anonymous data such as IP address (anonymized), browser type, pages visited, time spent, and device used. This data is used for statistical and security purposes.

8. Retention Period
